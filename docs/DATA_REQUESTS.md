# Data Requests for the Operator

Charter Addendum 01 section 2, point 5. When a dataset needed for a use-case
binding cannot be reached from the build environment, it is recorded here in
full rather than invented. Each entry carries everything the operator needs to
fetch it by hand, and each is surfaced at the next checkpoint.

A dataset is never fabricated to fill a row in this table. An empty binding
is an honest gap; a plausible-looking invented one is a fabricated benchmark.

---

## Open requests

### DR-001 | Bayanat REST API access (blocks all five Bayanat bindings)

**What is needed:** Access to the Bayanat open data portal REST API at
bayanat.ae to retrieve Resource GUIDs and download dataset resources for the
five MIZAN use cases.

**Portal:** https://bayanat.ae (Federal Competitiveness and Statistics
Centre, federal open data portal)

**Addendum citation:** Charter Addendum 01 section 2 describes the API:
`https://bayanat.ae/api/DatasetResources/GetDatasetResource?resourceID={ResourceGUID}`

**What was tried on 2026-08-19:**

Every API endpoint attempted returned HTTP 302 (Found) redirecting to
`/en/error/404`. The portal is built on Sitecore and uses antiforgery
tokens generated by browser JavaScript (`.AspNetCore.Antiforgery.*` cookie).
Unauthenticated server-side HTTP calls do not obtain these tokens and are
rejected before the API logic runs.

Specific endpoints that returned 302 to /en/error/404:
- `https://bayanat.ae/api/Dataset/GetAll?pageNo=1&pageSize=10&lang=en`
- `https://bayanat.ae/api/DatasetResources/GetDatasetResource?resourceID=test`
- `https://bayanat.ae/api/DatasetResources/GetAll?pageNo=1&pageSize=10`
- `https://bayanat.ae/api/Dataset/GetDatasets?pageNo=1&pageSize=20&topic=Government&subTopic=Service%20Centers`

The portal homepage (`https://bayanat.ae/en/`) returns HTTP 200 with the SPA
shell. The SPA loads data via JavaScript which curl and unauthenticated HTTP
clients cannot execute.

**What MIZAN does in the meantime:** All five use cases are bound to datasets
from the Ajman Open Data Portal (data.ajman.ae), which is accessible without
authentication and is explicitly named in the charter's network exception.
Those bindings are in `docs/evidence/data_sources.md` and are hash-verified.
The Bayanat bindings requested below would supplement or replace them.

**What the operator must do:**
1. Open a browser and navigate to https://bayanat.ae/en/Datasets.
2. Search for each dataset named in DR-002 through DR-006 below.
3. Open the dataset's Resource Information page.
4. Copy the Resource GUID (UUID format: xxxxxxxx-xxxx-xxxx-xxxx-xxxxxxxxxxxx).
5. Fetch the resource via:
   `https://bayanat.ae/api/DatasetResources/GetDatasetResource?resourceID={GUID}`
6. Save the response to `suites/data/bayanat-{dataset-slug}.json` and
   commit it alongside a manifest with the GUID and read date.
7. Update `docs/evidence/data_sources.md` with the Bayanat binding.

### DR-002 | Bayanat: Government Service Centre transactions (uc-001)

**Use case:** uc-001, Citizen-Facing Arabic Chatbot

**Ideal dataset:** A dataset recording citizen transactions at UAE government
service centres, by service type, language of interaction, and volume.
Publishing entities to search first: Federal Competitiveness and Statistics
Centre (604 datasets on bayanat.ae), Telecommunications and Digital
Government Regulatory Authority.

**Bayanat search terms:** "service centre" OR
"government services" OR "citizen transactions" in the Government topic,
Service Centres subcategory.

**Why this matters:** The Ajman Speed Centre dataset (da_vx5h92) grounds
the service knowledge dimension of the chatbot control set. A Bayanat dataset
from the FCSC or TDRA that records which services citizens actually request,
at what frequency, and in which language would additionally ground the volume
and language-mix calibration of the evaluation weights.

**Resource GUID:** not yet known; operator to retrieve from Resource
Information page.

**What to do with it:** Once obtained, add a second binding row to the uc-001
section of `docs/evidence/data_sources.md` with the Bayanat dataset name,
publisher, URL, GUID, and read date. Update the uc-001 grounding rationale to
note that both datasets are used.

### DR-003 | Bayanat: Government document inventory or publication register (uc-002)

**Use case:** uc-002, Internal Document Summarisation

**Ideal dataset:** A dataset recording the types and volumes of government
internal documents (circulars, policies, reports, decisions) produced by UAE
federal entities, ideally with Arabic document titles and category
classifications.

**Bayanat search terms:** "government documents" OR "official publications"
OR "circulars" OR "ministry reports" in the Government topic.

**Why this matters:** The Ajman HR Systems dataset (da_f8z46q) grounds the
policy document type dimension with 27 records. A federal-level dataset
covering a broader range of document types across multiple entities would
make the evaluation corpus more representative of what a deployed
summarisation model would actually encounter.

**Resource GUID:** not yet known.

### DR-004 | Bayanat: Social benefits or eligibility data (uc-003)

**Use case:** uc-003, Benefits Eligibility Triage

**Ideal dataset:** A dataset recording UAE social benefit categories,
eligibility criteria, or benefit application volumes across federal entities.
Ministry of Community Development or the General Pension and Social Security
Authority would be the expected publishers.

**Bayanat search terms:** "social benefits" OR "benefit eligibility" OR
"social support" OR "pension" in the Government or Health topic.

**Why this matters:** The Ajman benefit certificates dataset (da_hq5ypb)
covers one specific benefit type (rent contract certificates). A federal
dataset with multiple benefit categories and eligibility conditions would
calibrate the classification weights across the full set of benefits that a
UAE citizen might present.

**Resource GUID:** not yet known.

### DR-005 | Bayanat: Security and Justice incident data (uc-004)

**Use case:** uc-004, Traffic Incident Classification (more broadly: incident
classification including criminal, financial, and traffic types)

**Ideal dataset:** A dataset recording UAE incident report classifications
across multiple entities, ideally at federal or emirate-wide level rather than
a single police station.

**Bayanat search terms:** "incident reports" OR "crime statistics" OR
"traffic accidents" OR "police reports" in the Government topic, Security and
Justice subcategory.

**Why this matters:** The Ajman Police dataset (da_mew4ks) covers one
station's 39 months of classified reports. A federal or emirate-wide dataset
would provide a more representative class distribution for calibrating the
incident classification evaluation weights.

**Resource GUID:** not yet known.

### DR-006 | Bayanat: Government Budget or Open Procurement data (uc-005)

**Use case:** uc-005, Procurement Document Analysis

**Ideal dataset:** A dataset recording UAE government procurement contracts,
tenders, or budget expenditures by category. Ministry of Finance or the
General Authority for Government Procurement would be the expected publishers.
The addendum specifically names Government Budget and Open Procurement as
relevant subcategories.

**Bayanat search terms:** "procurement" OR "government contracts" OR
"tenders" OR "federal budget" OR "expenditure" in the Government topic,
Government Budget or Open Procurement subcategories.

**Why this matters:** The Ajman Chamber COO dataset (da_isigsw) grounds the
trade document structure dimension of procurement analysis. A federal
procurement dataset with tender documents, contract values, and supplier
categories would ground the full procurement lifecycle that the model is
evaluated against.

**Resource GUID:** not yet known.

## Format

Each request states: the use case it grounds, the dataset name as published
(or the search terms to find it), the publishing entity, the portal URL, the
Resource GUID if known, what specifically is needed from it, what was tried
and the exact failure, and what MIZAN does in the meantime.
